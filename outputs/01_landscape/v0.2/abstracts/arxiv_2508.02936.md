AQUAH is the first end-to-end language-based agent purpose-built for hydrologic modeling.
AQUAH stitches together geospatial data retrieval, Earth-observation forcing data,
hydrologic models (e.g., Coupled Routing and Excess Storage, crest), and automated
visualization in a seamless workflow. Starting with a simple natural-language prompt, AQUAH
autonomously retrieves the required terrain, forcing, and gauge data; configures a
hydrologic model; runs the simulation; and generates a self-contained PDF report. Across ten
independent initialisations tested on U.S. basins, the system produced analyst-ready
documentation that hydrologists judged as clear, transparent, and physically plausible,
though further calibration and validation are stated as still needed for operational
deployment.
