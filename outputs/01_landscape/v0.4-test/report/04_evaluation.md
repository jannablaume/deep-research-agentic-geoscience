# Evaluation

No shared benchmark appears among the three deep-read systems. Each defines its own test. [Certain]

The Devito translator reports Precision@5 / Recall@5 on eleven retrieval queries and a Grade-A rate on thirteen constructed finite-difference cases, with no external baseline named [[arxiv:2601.18381]]. [Certain]

TREMORS reports two qualitative workflow examples (event-based and continuous acquisition) and does not state a numeric comparison against a baseline [[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.01777]]. [Certain]

TRACE reports a hierarchical task benchmark and named-site applications (Ridgecrest 2019; Santorini–Kolumbo 2025), and compares LLM backbones (GPT-5, Claude-4, Gemini-3) plus manual and rule-based workflows [[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.21152]]. [Certain]

Abstract-only industry sources in this slice do not state evaluation protocols beyond the claim of a system [[doi:10.2118/229240-ms]] [[doi:10.2523/iptc-25122-ms]]. [Certain]
