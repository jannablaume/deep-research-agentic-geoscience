# 03 Applications

Source counts are admitted rows in `papers.csv` (core plus context).

## geomechanics

[Certain] One admitted source, context-tier only: Geo-Resource Agent
[[doi:10.56952/igs-2025-0391]]. No core-tier source remains in this subfield after
paywalled records were demoted.

## seismology

[Certain] Twenty-seven admitted sources, of which eight are core. The core set covers
catalog construction and physical reasoning (TRACE on Ridgecrest and Santorini–Kolumbo
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.21152]]), FDSN waveform retrieval (TREMORS
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.01777]]), SPECFEM configuration (specfem-mcp
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2512.14429]]), OpenQuake hazard (ESHM20-MCP
[[doi:10.1038/s44304-026-00262-z]]), GraphRAG over named catalogs
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.24984]], reconstruction-operator search (SeisEvo
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.18272]]), fault-segmentation NAS
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.13889]], and Fortran-to-Devito translation
[[arxiv:2601.18381]].

## hydrogeology

[Certain] Four admitted sources, all context after demotion. Hydro-Agent's Aquia Aquifer
inversion [[doi:10.1016/j.watres.2026.125886]] and the GAGAW hydrology workflow
[[doi:10.1016/j.bdes.2026.100042]] [[doi:10.22541/essoar.176336946.65126612/v1]] are
described in abstracts only. GAGAW's ESSOAr PDF returned HTTP 403 on retry; the GitHub
demonstration repository is not an evaluation section.

## reservoir_engineering

[Certain] Sixty-eight admitted sources, of which three are core: PetroGraph history matching
on SPE1, SPE9 and Norne [[arxiv:2605.15028]], GeoMind lithology classification on four
public well-log benchmarks [[arxiv:2604.21501]], and TADI question answering over Volve
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.00060]]. The remaining 65 are context, largely SPE, IPTC and
EAGE extended abstracts.

## geothermal

[Certain] Four admitted sources, one core: GAIA, evaluated on a homemade unpublished QA
set and a simplified synthetic inversion [[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2511.03852]].

## ccs

[Certain] Four admitted sources, two core. Agents4GEOS reproduces the PUNQ-S3 CO2
sequestration benchmark by calling GEOS [[arxiv:2607.18557]]. AutoSurrogate, recovered
from arXiv:2604.11945 after the Elsevier page was unread, trains pressure and saturation
surrogates on 1000 synthetic GEOS realisations of an unnamed 80×80×20 storage aquifer
[[doi:10.1016/j.aei.2026.105058]]. The remaining two are context (a life-cycle UQ
framework and the IEAGHG Agent George workshop note).

## mining

[Certain] Fourteen admitted sources, three core: Sim2Schedule on synthetic block models
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.10286]], MINDS on the Marvin copper benchmark
[[doi:10.3390/mining6020026]], and STA-CoT on MineBench
[[doi:10.18653/v1/2025.findings-emnlp.1386]].

## engineering_geology

[Certain] Nineteen admitted sources, four core: GeoMCP on a JRC Eurocode 7 worked example
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.01022]], LandslideAgent on LandslideBench
[[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18661]], the PPV Evaluator-Optimizer on a named Xiushan tunnel
[[doi:10.3390/geosciences16050176]], and the foundation-design router on 27 homemade cases
[[doi:10.1007/s43503-026-00088-8]].

## inversion

[Certain] Five admitted sources, none core. Well-log agents
[[doi:10.1016/j.petsci.2026.05.031]] [[doi:10.1016/s1876-3804(26)60734-3]] and GAGAW
[[doi:10.1016/j.bdes.2026.100042]] remain context. LogACF reports public datasets plus
unnamed tight-sandstone field data; the PED paper reports 100 unnamed field wells. Neither
evaluation section was readable.

## geological_modelling

[Certain] Nine admitted sources, two core: HERMES extracting the Treatise on Invertebrate
Paleontology into Treatise.geoLex [[doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.14055]] and the borehole-report
coordinate pipeline [[doi:10.1038/s41598-026-61824-9]]. AGeoKE
[[doi:10.1016/j.acags.2026.100362]] remains context: ScienceDirect was blocked; the Idaho
repository page repeats the abstract.

[Certain] Three of the ten core subfields have no full-text core source in this run:
geomechanics, hydrogeology and inversion. Those subfields are not empty of admitted
literature; they are empty of readable evaluation sections.
